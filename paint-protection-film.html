What is PPF?

Paint Protection Film (PPF) is a nearly invisible, durable polyurethane layer applied to your vehicle's painted surfaces. Designed to absorb impacts from road debris and everyday wear, PPF helps protect against rock chips, scratches, stains, bug splatter, and UV damage. Unlike a vinyl wrap, which is primarily used to change a vehicle's appearance, PPF is focused on preserving and protecting your factory paint while maintaining its original look.
